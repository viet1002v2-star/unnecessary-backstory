DRAWINGS NEEDED — drop finished files into this folder with exactly these names.
The page picks them up automatically. Until then:
  - the app icon is simply left out (no stand-in decoration);
  - the demo flowers show a dashed box with its file name and description.

Rules for every drawing
- One ink colour, line work only, no filled background.
- PNG with a transparent background (or scan on white and remove the white).
- Draw or scan at about 2x the size listed so lines stay sharp on phones.
- On the dark phone lock screen the app icon is turned white automatically.
