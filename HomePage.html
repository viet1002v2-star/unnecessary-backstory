The Unnecessary Backstory

Every gift has a story. This one has too many drafts.

Choosing a small gift sets off a stream of half-formed thoughts: ideas you drop, doubts that make no sense, and things that have nothing to do with the gift at all. Write them down here, privately. Then pick a few, put them in order, and send them along with the gift.

The person who opens your gift scans a code and reads your thoughts one notification at a time.

How it works

  1. Write privately

    Add thoughts as they come, with a picture or a file if you like. Nothing leaves this computer, and nobody sees anything until you choose.

  2. Choose what goes in the gift

    Tick the thoughts worth sharing and put them in the order you want them read. Everything else stays private.

  3. Wrap it up

    Add a last line, preview what they will see, and get a link or QR code to put with the gift.

Privacy

Your thoughts are saved only in this browser on this computer. There are no accounts.

A share link carries the words you chose. Pictures and files you add stay on this computer, and the site tells you before you share.
